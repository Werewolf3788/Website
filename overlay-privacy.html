Privacy notice — Werewolf Overlay

Last updated November 27, 2026 · covers the Werewolf Overlay Twitch extension and Overlay Studio. Run by werewolf3788.

If you're a viewer

  • The extension does not ask for, collect or store your name, Twitch username, email or location.
  • Twitch gives the extension an anonymous viewer ID. We don't save it.
  • What you see (the streamer's game, status, tracker) is sent to you by Twitch. Tapping “View more” asks our server for the streamer's public game tracker; the request only contains the channel you're watching.
  • We count page views and taps with Google Analytics (with IP anonymization). This gives totals like “how many people opened View more” — never who you are. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Tapping the “Overlay by werewolf3788” credit opens our website, where the site's own privacy policy applies.

If you're a streamer who installs it

  • From Twitch: your channel ID, channel name, profile picture, stream title, category, whether you're live and your viewer count — to build what your viewers see.
  • If you pair it with a Werewolf site account (optional): your friend code, the PlayStation online ID you saved on the site, your public game tracker, and your friends list (to show friends playing the same game).
  • If you buy a pass: your streaming account, the plan and the e-mail you paid with on Buy Me a Coffee. Payments are handled by Buy Me a Coffee; we never see card details.
  • This is stored in Google Firebase and used only to run the overlay and the extension. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.
  • Want it removed? Uninstall the extension and message werewolf3788 (or e-mail below) and we'll delete your channel's data.

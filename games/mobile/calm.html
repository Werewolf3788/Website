🔒 Private by design

Everything you put in Calm Space (check-ins, your reasons, your plan, your people) is saved only on this device. It is never uploaded, never shown to friends, and not tied to your account. Clearing your browser data erases it, so make a backup file now and then.

🏮 Breathe: light the lantern

Follow the circle. Breathe in while it grows and out, slowly, while it shrinks. A longer breath out tells your body it's safe to settle down. Six slow breaths light the lantern.

When it helps

Use this when your heart is racing, your chest is tight, you're about to snap at someone, or you can't sleep. Two minutes is enough to notice a change. If you feel lightheaded, slow down and breathe normally for a moment.

✨ Ground: find the fireflies

When your mind is spinning, pull it back into the room you're in. For each step, look around and tap Found one each time you notice something. You can type it if that helps (nothing you type is saved).

Why it works

Panic, flashbacks and spiralling thoughts live in the past or the future. Naming what your senses pick up right now (5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste) gives your brain something real and present to hold on to.

🪨 Cool down: stack the cairn

Tap (or Space / controller ✕) to drop each stone. It stays as long as its middle lands on the stone below. Every stone you place slows the next one down. Twelve stones finish the cairn.

Monthly and yearly

A month is 31 days and a year is 365 days. They don't renew on their own. Paying again while a pass is active adds the time on to the end. Moving to another tier keeps any time you have left on the old one.

Lifetime

One payment, for the lifetime of the service — as long as Overlay Studio and the Werewolf Twitch extension are running. Upgrading to a higher lifetime tier only costs the difference from what you already paid for lifetime.

Founder lifetime (first 25 buyers)

A cheaper lifetime pass for the first 25 buyers. In return, a small werewolf3788 credit (profile picture and name) shows on your stream about once a month, for a few seconds, once your stream has been live at least 5 minutes and at least 2 people are watching. If a stream doesn't reach that, it waits for the next one. Missed months don't add up. You agree to this with the checkbox before you pay; your agreement is saved with your purchase.

Full lifetime buyers never see the credit.

Paying

Payments go through Buy Me a Coffee with the code the page gives you. If anything goes wrong, message werewolf with your Buy Me a Coffee receipt.

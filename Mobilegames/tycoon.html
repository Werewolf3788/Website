📜 Win / loss history
Everyone starts with $1,500 and collects $200 every time you pass or land on Start. Taxes, fines and bail go into the Free Parking pot, and whoever lands on Free Parking collects it. Buy what you land on, collect rent, and build up to 4 houses and then an apartment once you own a whole colour. Doubles roll again; three doubles go to jail. Rents rise by 25% every 4 rounds in long games. Last player standing wins. Tap the dice, shake your phone or press Space to roll. A table only runs while at least one human is playing: if the last person leaves, the game ends. If someone leaves while others stay, a bot takes their seat. A game only counts toward your record if you played it from the first move to the last.

🎮 Werewolf Mobile Games

Games for phones and computers. Play solo any time, or sign in and share your friend code so friends can join you.

🌾 Neighborly Acre

A 3D farm: crops, animals, a tractor, missions and levels. Solo is your private farm. In a multiplayer farm (up to 8 players) everyone starts fresh with their own field and progress, and only the animals are shared.

🎯 Wahoo

2 to 4 players. Race four pegs around the board, bump rivals back home, fill your home lane first. Bots fill empty seats.

🏙️ Monopoly

2 to 4 players. Buy streets, stations and utilities, collect rent, build houses and bankrupt your rivals. Bots fill empty seats.

🐸 Frogger

Cross the road, ride the logs over the river, then dodge the swamp predators. Solo is endless with levels. Multiplayer is a race for up to 8 players: everyone sees the same hazards, first to 5 crossings wins.

🚢 Battleship

The classic: hide five ships on a 10 × 10 grid, then take turns calling shots like D3 or B9 by tapping the square. Cannon, splash, hit and sinking sounds, and an optional voice that calls the shots. Play the computer or a friend (2 players).

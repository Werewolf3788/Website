Owner-only. These values are never shown to friends. They hide automatically 60 seconds after you reveal them.
To get your PSN Account ID

Open psn.flipscreen.games, type your PlayStation username and copy the long number it shows (the Account ID, usually 19 digits). Paste it in the Account ID box above.

How to get your NPSSO

1. Sign in to PlayStation in this browser. 2. Open ca.account.sony.com/api/v1/ssocookie. 3. Paste everything it shows, or only the 64-character value. We keep just the token.

Steam

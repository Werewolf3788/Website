Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform, companion apps, or sign in through Firebase Authentication, we may collect the following information:

  • Authentication Identifiers: Email addresses, telephone numbers (used strictly for single-use SMS verification), and third-party authentication tokens (such as Google Sign-In).
  • User Profile Data: Custom display gamer tags, preferred gaming platform (PlayStation, PC, Xbox), avatar image links, and generated Friend Codes.
  • Game & Telemetry State: Harvest logs, trophy ratings, discovered need zones, dog skill allocations, checklist progression, and active challenge markers synced to Cloud Firestore and browser localStorage.

2. How We Use and Share Information

Your information is used strictly to provide core gaming tools and synchronization features across your devices:

  • To verify identity and persist your personal hunting progress across browser refreshes and sessions.
  • To permit companion social features: when a player connects using your Friend Code, they can view your chosen display gamer tag, primary platform, and game progress metrics. Your private login details (email and phone number) are never exposed to other players.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal user data to any third-party advertisers.

3. Data Deletion & Self-Service Account Closure

Immediate Self-Service Deletion: You retain complete control over your stored records. You can permanently erase your entire profile, trophy logs, and cloud documents at any time by navigating to your Account Settings and clicking "Permanently Delete Account".

Upon confirming deletion, your Firestore user document tree is wiped immediately and your Firebase Authentication record is revoked.

Alternatively, you may request manual data purge by contacting us directly at raykevin71888@gmail.com. Manual requests are processed within 48 hours.

4. Local Storage & Offline Usage

If you use the portal without signing in, all application state (harvests, filters, and preferences) is stored locally on your device within browser localStorage. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding your privacy or data handling, reach out to the project administrator at raykevin71888@gmail.com.

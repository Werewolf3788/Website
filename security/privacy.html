Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in and interact with our platform, companion apps, or synchronization tools across Firebase, we collect and process the following information:

  • Primary Authentication Identifiers: Your verified email address (resolved with precedence given to your Google OAuth profile if linked, or your standard registration address). Email addresses serve as the unique document identifier in Cloud Firestore (users/{email}) and as the sanitized root key in Firebase Realtime Database (/users/{emailKey}).
  • Public & Shared Hunter Profile: Your custom display name/gamer tag, selected avatar preference (Google profile image, live Twitch DecAPI avatar, or custom URL), connected social links (capped at 5 personal shortcuts), and an auto-generated 8-character Friend Code.
  • Gaming Handles & Telemetry Identifiers: Public and friend-scoped usernames for PlayStation Network, Steam, and Xbox Live, as well as Twitch handles used to query streaming state via DecAPI.
  • Hunting Progress & Companion Logs: Harvest records, trophy ratings, watchlists, cull progression, herd management lifecycles, and dog skill allocations recorded in Cloud Firestore.

2. Strictly Private & Sensitive Credentials

Owner-Only Security Isolation: Backend telemetry keys—specifically your PSN Account ID # and your PSN NPSSO Session Cookie—are classified as strictly private credentials.

  • Zero Public Exposure: These sensitive keys are locked to No One (Private) and are never shared with friends, displayed on public badges, or leaked in search queries. They are stored solely in your private RTDB record to authenticate background trophy synchronization workers.
  • In-Browser Auto-Masking: On the Settings dashboard, sensitive credentials default to masked password fields and feature a 60-second timed reveal safeguard before automatically re-masking to prevent unauthorized over-the-shoulder viewing.
  • Input Sanitization: NPSSO payloads pasted as raw Sony API responses are parsed client-side to isolate the bare 64-character token; expired session error responses are detected without storing unauthorized session tokens.

3. How Information is Used and Shared

We process your data strictly to maintain core game companion utilities and multi-device synchronizations:

  • Telemetry & Session Persistence: Your settings, live presence, and linked platforms stay synced in real time across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
  • Friend-Gated Telemetry Access: When another hunter exchanges an 8-character Friend Code with you, they receive access only to your display name, platform badge, and hunting logs. Your private email address and backend credentials remain completely invisible to companions.
  • Third-Party Microservices: When utilizing live Twitch lookups, your channel handle queries public DecAPI endpoints (decapi.me/twitch/game/ and decapi.me/twitch/avatar/) to retrieve game titles and avatar artwork. No authentication tokens are transmitted to DecAPI.
  • No Data Monetization: We never sell, lease, track, or share personal information with third-party advertising networks.

4. Data Erasure & Self-Service Account Closure

Full Self-Service Erasure: You retain complete ownership over your cloud data. You can permanently delete your entire profile and cloud telemetry at any time via Settings > Delete Account & Erase Cloud Records.

Executing account deletion performs an immediate, simultaneous purge across our cloud architecture:

  • Erases your settings node and linked handles from Firebase Realtime Database at /users/{emailKey}.
  • Removes your permanent lookup token from /friend_codes/{code}.
  • Purges your user document and companion subcollections from Cloud Firestore at users/{email}.
  • Revokes and deletes your master Firebase Authentication record.

Alternatively, you may request manual data purge by contacting our administration directly at tryouts-96-billet@icloud.com. Manual requests are processed within 48 hours.

5. Local Storage & Client Cache

For visitors using our tools without signing in, configuration data (such as active filters, local checklists, and view toggles) is retained entirely in browser localStorage. This data never touches our servers and can be wiped instantly by clearing browser site data.

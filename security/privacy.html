1. What This Policy Covers

This policy covers everything in the Werewolf Project website (the Werewolf3788/Website repository), served at entertainment-71888.web.app and on GitHub Pages, plus the helper services and scheduled jobs that run for it. That includes:

  • Accounts & Settings: sign-in (Google, email, Discord), the Settings page, Friend Codes and linked gaming accounts.
  • Social: user profile pages, friends and friend requests, the site-wide messenger (including the Discord server bridge), online status and achievement pop-ups.
  • Game stats & trackers: PlayStation, Xbox and Steam stats; the Game Companion for every supported game (theHunter: Call of the Wild, Way of the Hunter, Sniper Elite, Ghost Recon Wildlands, Far Cry, Watch Dogs Legion, Ghost of Tsushima and more); and the Farming Simulator 25 server dashboard (until the server shuts down on November 24, 2026).
  • Browser games: the mobile games hub (Neighborly Acre co-op farm, Wahoo, Battleship, Frogger, Mahjong, Racing, Survival, Tycoon and others).
  • Streaming tools: stream overlays, user stream pages, the stream sync service (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok) and the trophy announcement chat bot.
  • Entertainment & extras: Werewolf Cinema (movies and TV), Music releases, the Deals page, the Subscription & Renewal Tracker, Ray's Monopoly Vault, and the site's install-as-app and notification features.

It is a personal, hobby-run project. "We" and "us" mean the site owner (werewolf). Partner sites linked from the menu (SK Ventures, SMLC, Quick-Links, StreamElements, SoundCloud, Discord) have their own privacy policies.

2. Information We Collect

What we store depends on which features you use:

  • Account: Your email address and the sign-in methods you link (Google, email and password, or Discord). Your email is the key for your records in our database. With Discord sign-in, our login server receives your Discord user ID, username and avatar and saves a link between your Discord ID and your account.
  • Profile: Display name/gamer tag, avatar (Google photo, Twitch avatar, or a custom link), up to 5 personal links, theme, colors and language choices, profile page bio and background, and an auto-generated 8-character Friend Code.
  • Linked gaming accounts: Your PlayStation Network, Xbox gamertag (and Xbox user ID), Steam and Twitch names, plus the trophies, achievements, play time, recent games and online status our sync jobs fetch for them.
  • Game Companion & trackers: Progress you record for each game, such as harvest logs, trophy ratings, watchlists, herd management, checklists and collectibles.
  • Friends & messages: Friend requests, your friends list, and private messages you send in the site messenger. If you use the Discord pill in the messenger, messages you post there are sent to that Discord server's channel.
  • Online status: Whether you are online and which page or game room you are in, so friends can see you and join games.
  • Browser games: Game saves, shared game rooms (such as the co-op farm), leaderboard scores, and achievement announcements with your display name and avatar, shown to everyone who has the site open.
  • Streaming tools (streamers who turn them on): Your stream sync settings (channels, tags, squad list, title options) and the chats you choose to relay. While you are live, chat messages and usernames from your YouTube and TikTok chat are passed into your Twitch chat. They are relayed, not stored.
  • Farming Simulator 25 server: The dashboard reads the G-Portal server's save files, including which players are online and their farm progress. The server shuts down on November 24, 2026. After that, no new FS25 data is collected. Data already saved stays on the dashboard unless you ask us to remove it.
  • Wi-Fi sharing (optional): Network names and passwords you save in Settings, with the sharing level you choose.
  • Push notifications (optional): If you turn notifications on, your browser's push subscription (a device address, not your name or email).
  • Subscription Tracker & Monopoly Vault: These pages read lists from Google Sheets and JSON files kept by their owners. Visitors' data isn't collected there beyond sign-in.

3. Private Credentials

Owner-only: Keys and tokens you give the site are stored in a locked part of the database (user_secrets) that only you and our background jobs can read. They are never shown to friends or on profiles.

These include:

  • PlayStation Account ID and NPSSO session cookie.
  • Steam Web API key and OpenXBL (Xbox) key.
  • For streamers: Twitch, Kick and bot login tokens, a Discord webhook link, and YouTube hook settings.
  • Masked on screen: In Settings these show as hidden fields, with a 60-second reveal that re-hides automatically.
  • Cleaned on paste: If you paste a full Sony response instead of the bare NPSSO token, the page pulls out just the token in your browser. Expired-session responses are not saved.
  • Owner's keys as a fallback: If you don't add your own Steam key, the site owner's key is used to look up your public stats.

4. Where Your Data Is Kept

  • Main database: Google Firebase project entertainment-71888 (Realtime Database, Cloud Firestore and sign-in), run by the site owner.
  • PlayStation & Xbox stats: The PlayStation and Xbox sync jobs save game stats to a second Firebase project (playstation-stats), run by a co-owner of the site.
  • Public website files: Some game data is saved as files in the public GitHub repository and website, so anyone can view it. This includes PlayStation trophy and game stats for linked players (PSN name, account ID, games, trophies and play time) and the Farming Simulator 25 server data.
  • Your device: Settings, filters, checklists and offline game saves kept in your browser (see section 7).

5. How We Use & Share Information

  • Running the site: Keeping your settings, linked accounts and progress synced across your devices, and running the games, messenger and stats pages.
  • Friends: People you exchange Friend Codes with can see your display name, avatar, platform badges, online status, game stats and tracker logs. Your email and private credentials are never shown to friends.
  • Public pages: Profile pages, user stream pages, leaderboards, achievement pop-ups and game stats pages can be viewed by other visitors, using your display name and gamer tags rather than your email.
  • Services that help run the site:
    • Google Firebase, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script; GitHub Pages and GitHub Actions (hosting and scheduled sync jobs); Render (Discord login and stream sync servers).
    • Sony PlayStation, Xbox (through OpenXBL) and Steam, which our sync jobs contact with your handles and keys to fetch your stats.
    • Discord, for sign-in, the messenger bridge and live alerts.
    • Twitch, YouTube, Kick and TikTok, for streamers who turn on stream sync, chat relay or the trophy bot.
    • DecAPI (decapi.me), which gets your public Twitch name to look up your current game and avatar.
    • G-Portal, which hosts the Farming Simulator 25 server until November 24, 2026.
    • Images and fonts loaded from Steam, PlayStation, Twitch and Google servers, which can see your IP address when your browser fetches them.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never give your email, messages or private credentials to advertisers.

6. Advertising, Analytics & Affiliate Links

  • Google AdSense: Pages on this site show ads served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ad Settings, or opt out of many third-party ad cookies at aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It collects things like pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location, using cookies. It isn't linked to your account email. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Amazon affiliate links: As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. Links on the Deals page, Werewolf Cinema and game pages may go to Amazon, and Amazon may set its own cookies when you click them. Buying through them costs you nothing extra.
  • Donations: Tips go through StreamElements. We don't see or store your payment details.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

The site uses browser storage (localStorage) to keep your filters, checklists, view choices, theme and offline game saves on your device, and a small offline cache so pages load faster when installed as an app. If you aren't signed in, this data stays on your device and never reaches our servers. Clearing your browser's site data removes it. Firebase sign-in also keeps you logged in using browser storage. Advertising and analytics cookies are described in section 6.

8. Keeping & Deleting Your Data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete it at any time.

Self-service deletion: Go to Settings > Delete account & erase cloud records.

This immediately removes:

  • Your settings and linked handles.
  • Your private credentials.
  • Your Friend Code.
  • Your main Cloud Firestore user record.
  • Your sign-in account.

Everything else is removed on request: messages, profile page, game saves and leaderboard scores, stream sync settings, shared Wi-Fi entries, push subscriptions, and PlayStation/Xbox stats in the second database and public files. Email us (section 12) and we'll delete everything tied to your account within 48 hours. Messages you sent stay visible to the other person until then. Messages posted to Discord through the bridge are removed in Discord itself.

9. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected by Firebase sign-in and database rules that limit who can read each record. Private credentials are readable only by you and our background jobs. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong password and keep your NPSSO cookie, API keys and tokens to yourself.

11. Changes to This Policy

We update this policy when the site adds or changes features. The effective date at the top and the build stamp at the bottom show when it last changed. Continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the new version.
